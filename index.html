How we built it

    Limits to keep in mind

    • The postings are a Kaggle snapshot of LinkedIn from January 2024, so they show one moment of the market and only roles labeled as data science.
    • Skills were already extracted in the dataset. Our dictionary decides which variants count as the same skill, and those choices shape the network.
    • Modularity is modest, so the clusters overlap. Machine Learning sits in the middle and ties all three together.
    • Thresholds (30 postings per skill, 10 shared postings per link) drop rare skills and pairs.
